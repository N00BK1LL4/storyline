## Description

Storyline gives your staff one panel to run stories in Night City.

**World Events.** Bosses that scale with players, squads, AV crashes, objects falling from
the sky, supply drops, and the Chimera.

**Custom Scenes.** Characters your staff speak through, bookmarks, shared objectives and
things players can hack, search or blow up.

**Fits your server.** Connect your own inventory, announcements, objective and interaction
systems, or call your own scripts at 19 different moments. Rights per feature, and setup
that only the owner can change.

**This is a public test.** Parts have only been tested with simulated players. Please run it
on a test server and tell us what breaks.

## Needs

`open77_notifications`. Optional: `open77_props` (craters, objects), `op77_map3d` (map),
`open77_loot` (ground loot). The Chimera needs Phantom Liberty for every player fighting it.
